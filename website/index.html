The Problem We Solve

Health insurance schemes need to know if providers are contracted before paying claims

📊

Spreadsheets Cause Leakage

Claims paid outside contract scope, fee schedules drift, and no audit trail of provider credentialing.

🔍

Lack of Accountability

No visibility into why providers were approved/rejected, creating risks during audits and transitions.

⏰

Missed Renewals

Contracts lapse silently without reminders or grace periods, disrupting care for beneficiaries.

Comprehensive Solution

End-to-end provider contract management built for openIMIS

01

Multi-Stage Empanelment

Configurable workflows from application to committee decision with document checklists and tamper-evident audit logs.

02

Contract Lifecycle

Manage drafts, negotiations, signatures, renewals, and terminations with full amendment versioning and effective dating.

03

Per-Contract Fee Schedules

Effective-dated pricing that materializes into openIMIS pricelists—no parallel pricing engine needed.

04

Smart Claims Gate

Read-only validation flags out-of-scope claims for review without blocking care—advisory, never punitive.

About openIMIS

openIMIS is a Digital Public Good that helps governments and organizations implement and manage social protection and health financing programs. It's used by countries across Africa, Asia, and beyond to provide affordable, accessible healthcare coverage.
